Who I Am

The full picture

For the past 19 months I've been the embedded brand marketing lead for BW Restaurants Inc. — Boardwalk Burgers and Lava Grill — operating across 23 Canadian franchise locations. I sit inside their brand team, reporting directly to the Brand Director and CEO.

I own the influencer program end-to-end: strategy, creator sourcing and contracting, briefing, content review, paid integration, event production, and reporting. I don't support campaigns — I design them and run them.

I've also built the operational systems behind the scenes: ClickUp automation workflows that notify franchisees the moment an influencer is booked, ticketing systems for 23 locations, and CRM migrations that kept the franchise development pipeline running without a beat missed.

Currently also building InfluenceMap on the side — a creator discovery and outreach automation tool — because I genuinely believe AI will reshape how influencer marketing works at scale.

Sweet Heat 🌶️

A brief to launch one burger became a full 360° campaign. On-set, I pitched and won approval for a dual product line — sweet & spicy chicken and beef — a Canadian first. The concept, tagline, and positioning were entirely mine.

The campaign ran across print, in-store, social, paid Meta & TikTok, and a live influencer launch event — 15 creators, a DJ, branded decor, and gift hampers — with co-brand compliance managed across both Tabasco and Coca-Cola.
